\paragraph{Limitations.}
Our method relies on a frozen SAM-3 ViT-H backbone, making inference memory-intensive
($\sim$10~GB GPU at $512{\times}512$). The staged training requires two sequential phases,
adding wall-clock time relative to all-at-once training.
Evaluation is single-seed (seed~0); multi-seed variance has not been characterised.
The $\text{fpn}_0$ residual alpha is initialised small ($\sigma(\alpha)\approx 0.05$)
to prevent early collapse, but the optimal schedule may be dataset-dependent.
GlaS results use $224{\times}224$ fixed resize, which may lose fine-grained gland boundary
information; adaptive-resolution extensions are left for future work.
